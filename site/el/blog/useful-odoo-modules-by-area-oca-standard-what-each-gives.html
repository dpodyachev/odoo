Odoo modules that make your ERP more powerful: a practical list by area

Το άρθρο δεν είναι ακόμη διαθέσιμο στα ελληνικά – ακολουθεί η αγγλική έκδοση.

Concrete modules for Odoo 17 Community and Enterprise, sorted by area – audit log, background jobs, test-database ribbon, management reports, bank reconciliation, assets, DATEV, stock rules, helpdesk, documents, mail tracking: what each one gives, when you do not need it, and how to install an OCA module in docker.

Odoo out of the box covers a lot, but many companies run into the same gaps: who changed this price, why is the import blocking everybody, where is the bank reconciliation in Community, how do I get a real P&L by month. For most of these gaps there is already a well-maintained module – usually from the Odoo Community Association (OCA). Below is our short list by area, with one honest column: when you do not need the module.

All OCA module names below were checked in the 17.0 branches on github.com/OCA on 25 November 2026. Availability for 18.0 and 19.0 differs per module – check the branch of your version. We are an independent Odoo service provider, not an Odoo partner.

Before you install anything

Every module you add has to be ported and tested again at every Odoo upgrade. Where modules come from, what their licences mean and a checklist for third-party code are in our separate article Odoo apps and modules. Here it is only about which modules are worth that price. Rule number one stays: install on a copy of the database first.

Administration and operations

ModuleRepositoryWhat it givesNot needed when
auditlogOCA/server-toolsA log of who changed which field when, with old and new value – per model you choose.nobody ever asks “who changed this?” – or you only need it on one or two fields, where standard chatter tracking is enough.
database_cleanupOCA/server-toolsFinds leftovers of uninstalled modules: orphan tables, columns, models – and lets you remove them.the database was created fresh and no module was ever uninstalled. Use only on a copy and with a backup.
auto_backupOCA/server-toolsScheduled database dumps from inside Odoo, optionally to a remote server.you already back up with pg_dump outside Odoo – which we recommend: a backup should not depend on the application it protects.
base_technical_userOCA/server-toolsA technical user for crons and integrations instead of running them as the admin.you have no integrations and no own scheduled jobs.

auditlog is the one we install most often. Keep the list of logged models short – logging every write on stock.move or account.move.line makes the log table grow fast and slows down busy days.

Background jobs: queue_job

queue_job (repository OCA/queue) moves long tasks into the background: imports, mass e-mails, synchronisation with a shop, exports to accounting. Users do not wait, failed jobs can be retried, and you see every job with its error. Many other OCA modules depend on it.

  • It must be loaded server-wide: server_wide_modules = web,queue_job in odoo.conf, and Odoo must run with workers (workers > 0).
  • Set the channels, e.g. [queue_job] channels = root:2 – otherwise one heavy import can still occupy all workers.
  • With several databases on one server, the job runner needs db_name or a proper dbfilter – the classic trap we describe in the article on list_db = False.
  • Not needed when nothing in your system runs longer than a few seconds.

Usability

ModuleRepositoryWhat it givesNot needed when
web_environment_ribbonOCA/webA coloured ribbon “TEST” on every non-production database – configurable via system parameters.you never have a test copy. You should have one.
web_responsiveOCA/webA mobile-friendly app menu and layout for the backend.you are on Odoo 17 or newer: the standard backend is responsive already. Useful mainly on 16 and older.
web_m2x_optionsOCA/webControls “Create and edit…” and the number of entries in many2one dropdowns.users never create half-filled records by mistake from a dropdown.
partner_firstnameOCA/partner-contactSeparate first and last name fields for contacts.you only work with companies, or the single name field is fine for your letters and exports.

Reports and figures

ModuleRepositoryWhat it givesNot needed when
mis_builderOCA/mis-builderManagement reports defined by rules: P&L by month, budget vs actual, KPIs by department – exportable to Excel.you are on Enterprise and the standard financial reports and budgets are enough.
account_financial_reportOCA/account-financial-reportingTrial balance, general ledger, open items, aged partner balance – for Community.you are on Enterprise: the full accounting reports are included.
report_xlsxOCA/reporting-engineA base for Excel exports of any report – developers build on it.nobody needs Excel files that are generated by Odoo itself.
date_rangeOCA/server-uxNamed periods (months, quarters, fiscal years) to pick in reports.you only report by calendar month.

mis_builder needs date_range and report_xlsx, which is why they appear together here. It is the one module that most often replaces a weekly Excel export.

Accounting in Community – and for Germany

In Community, Odoo ships invoicing and basic accounting; the bank reconciliation widget, asset management and most reports are Enterprise. OCA fills exactly these gaps. For Germany, the standard localisation l10n_de with the SKR03/SKR04 charts of accounts comes with Odoo; OCA/l10n-germany adds the rest:

ModuleRepositoryWhat it givesNot needed when
account_reconcile_ocaOCA/account-reconcileA bank reconciliation screen for Community: match statement lines with invoices and payments.you are on Enterprise – its own reconciliation is included.
account_statement_import_camtOCA/bank-statement-importImport of CAMT.053 bank statements (the format German banks provide).your bank is synchronised online, or you are on Enterprise with its own import.
account_asset_managementOCA/account-financial-toolsFixed assets with depreciation boards and automatic depreciation entries.you are on Enterprise (Assets app), or your tax advisor keeps the assets outside Odoo.
datev_exportOCA/l10n-germanyExport of journal entries in DATEV format for the tax advisor.the tax advisor works directly in Odoo, or you are on Enterprise with the DATEV export – see our DATEV article.
l10n_de_tax_statementOCA/l10n-germanyThe German VAT return (Umsatzsteuer-Voranmeldung) figures from Odoo.the tax advisor prepares the VAT return from the DATEV data anyway.

Warehouse

The standard Inventory app already covers routes, multi-step receipts and lots. These OCA modules close small but annoying gaps:

ModuleRepositoryWhat it givesNot needed when
stock_no_negativeOCA/stock-logistics-workflowForbids negative stock per product or category.negative stock is never an issue – or you deliberately allow it for drop-ship-like flows.
stock_picking_invoice_linkOCA/stock-logistics-workflowLinks deliveries and invoices both ways: which picking was invoiced where.you invoice exactly by order and nobody asks which delivery an invoice belongs to.
stock_cycle_countOCA/stock-logistics-warehouseCycle counting: count parts of the warehouse regularly instead of one big inventory per year.the warehouse is small enough to count completely in a day.
stock_mts_mto_ruleOCA/stock-logistics-warehouseMixed rule “take from stock, order the rest”.you either always buy to order or always deliver from stock.

Service, documents, e-mail

ModuleRepositoryWhat it givesNot needed when
helpdesk_mgmtOCA/helpdeskTickets with teams, stages, SLA-like deadlines and a customer portal – for Community.you are on Enterprise and use the Helpdesk app.
dmsOCA/dmsA document management system: folders, access rights, files linked to records.you are on Enterprise (Documents app), or files live in a separate system such as SharePoint or Nextcloud.
mail_trackingOCA/socialShows whether an e-mail was delivered, opened or bounced – right in the chatter.your mail server or e-mail provider already reports this and nobody looks inside Odoo.

How to install an OCA module

OCA repositories are plain git repositories, one per topic, one branch per Odoo version. You clone the branch of your version and add the folder to addons_path. In docker it looks like this:

  • Always clone the branch of your Odoo version (17.0, 18.0, …). A module from another branch will not install or, worse, will half work.
  • Many modules depend on modules from other OCA repositories – the manifest depends tells you which repositories you also need.
  • Pin the exact commit you tested (or keep the repositories in your own git) – git pull on production without a test is how updates break.
  • Remove a module the same way: uninstall on the copy first, check, then on production.

What we would install first

  1. web_environment_ribbon on every test copy – it costs nothing and prevents “oops, that was production”.
  2. auditlog on a few critical models: prices, partners' bank accounts, users and access rights.
  3. queue_job as soon as anything runs longer than a minute: imports, shop sync, mass mailing.
  4. account_reconcile_oca and account_statement_import_camt for Community with German banks.
  5. mis_builder when someone builds the same Excel report every month.

Conclusion

Modules are cheap to install and expensive to keep. The ones above solve problems that almost every Odoo company meets, they are widely used and maintained for current versions. Install them on a copy, keep the list short, and write down why each module is there – then the next upgrade is a plan, not a surprise.
