E-mail in Odoo does not work: the checklist

Quotes do not arrive, replies do not show up in Odoo, mails land in spam? A checklist in two parts: what any user can check in two minutes, and what the administrator checks – with the typical causes and what to send to support.

"The mail does not work" can mean four different things: Odoo does not send, the mail is sent but lands in spam or bounces, incoming mails do not reach Odoo, or replies end up in the wrong place. Each has its own cause – the checklist below separates them.

Applies to Odoo 16–19, Community and Enterprise, own server and Odoo Online. Menu paths as in Odoo 18; the developer mode is needed for the Technical menu.

Quick check (2 minutes, any user)

  1. Open the document (quote, invoice) and look at the chatter at the bottom: a red envelope next to the message means sending failed – click it, the reason is shown.
  2. Was the address of the recipient correct? A typo is the most common cause.
  3. Ask the recipient to look in the spam folder.
  4. A grey envelope = still in the queue. Wait 2–3 minutes and reload the page.
  5. Did it work yesterday? Note what changed: new password, new mailbox, new domain, update.

For the administrator

  1. Settings ‣ Technical ‣ Emails – filter *Delivery Failed*: the column *Failure Reason* contains the server's answer. Retry sends again after the fix.
  2. Settings ‣ Technical ‣ Outgoing Mail Servers ‣ *Test Connection* – a failed test shows the same message as the mail server.
  3. Settings ‣ Technical ‣ Automation ‣ Scheduled Actions – *Mail: Email Queue Manager* must be active; on your own server cron must run (max_cron_threads not 0).
  4. Incoming: Settings ‣ Technical ‣ Incoming Mail Servers – status *Confirmed*, *Fetch Now* shows errors; check the alias domain under Settings ‣ General Settings.
  5. Spam and bounces: check the domain's SPF, DKIM and DMARC records – the sending server must be allowed in SPF.

What to send us

  • the document and time (e.g. "quote S00042, today 10:15") and the recipient's address;
  • the red envelope message or the failure reason as text or screenshot;
  • what changed recently (password, mailbox, domain, update);
  • never passwords or app passwords in chat or e-mail – we will ask for access separately.
