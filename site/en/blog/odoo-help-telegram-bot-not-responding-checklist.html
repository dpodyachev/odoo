The Telegram bot stopped responding: the checklist

The bot no longer sends notifications or does not answer commands? What a user checks in two minutes, what the administrator checks – getMe, getWebhookInfo, process and logs – and the typical causes: revoked token, a second copy with the same token, a group that became a supergroup.

Telegram bots around Odoo send notifications (new lead, failed backup, server alarm) or answer commands. When one falls silent, the cause is almost always in one of three places: in Telegram (chat, rights, token), in the bot's process on the server, or in the connection between them.

Applies to bots in polling and webhook mode, whether they run in Docker, as a systemd service or under pm2. The token is a password: it belongs in the bot's configuration only.

Quick check (2 minutes, any user)

  1. Send the bot /start or another command in a private chat. If it answers there, the problem is in the group, not in the bot.
  2. Did you block or mute the bot? Telegram ‣ chat with the bot ‣ profile ‣ Unblock / Unmute.
  3. Group: is the bot still a member? Was the group recently turned into a supergroup or a topic forum (by adding admins, history, topics)?
  4. Does Telegram work otherwise for you (other chats)?
  5. When did the last message from the bot arrive? This time narrows the search in the logs.

For the administrator

  1. getMe – the token is valid and the bot exists; 401 Unauthorized means the token was revoked or regenerated in BotFather.
  2. getWebhookInfo – with a webhook: last_error_message shows why Telegram cannot deliver; a growing pending_update_count means updates are piling up.
  3. Process: is it running, since when, did it restart in a loop? The last 100 log lines usually show the exact error.
  4. Server: disk full, memory exhausted, outgoing connections to api.telegram.org blocked – the bot dies silently.
  5. After a restore or a stage copy: does a second copy run with the same token? Telegram then answers 409 Conflict to one of them.

Typical causes

Symptom / messageCauseFix
401 Unauthorizedtoken revoked or regenerated in BotFathernew token into the configuration, restart the bot

403 Forbidden: bot was blocked by the userthe user blocked the botunblock and send /start

What to send us

  • the bot's name (@…) and the chat or group where it fell silent – not the token;
  • when the last message came and what the bot should have sent;
  • whether something changed: new group, new admins, server move, restore, update;
  • a screenshot of the error, if the bot answered with one.
