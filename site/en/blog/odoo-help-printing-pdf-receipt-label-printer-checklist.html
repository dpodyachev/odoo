Printing in Odoo does not work: the checklist for PDFs, receipts and labels

The invoice PDF is empty or without logo, the till does not print receipts, labels come out as gibberish? Odoo prints in three different ways – the checklist separates them: what a user checks in two minutes, what the administrator checks, typical causes and what to send to support.

Odoo prints in three different ways, and each breaks differently: PDF documents (quotes, invoices, delivery notes) are created on the server with wkhtmltopdf and printed by your browser; receipts at the till go directly from the browser to a network receipt printer (Epson ePOS) or through an IoT box; labels and direct printing to a specific printer go through the IoT box.

Applies to Odoo 16–19. On Odoo Online and Odoo.sh the PDF engine is maintained by Odoo – there only the browser, the layout and the devices in your shop are left to check.

Quick check (2 minutes, any user)

  1. PDF: does the document open as PDF (Print ‣ the file opens or downloads)? If yes, the problem is the printer or the browser's print dialog, not Odoo.
  2. Did the browser block a pop-up (icon in the address bar)? Allow pop-ups for the Odoo address, or try another browser.
  3. Receipt printer: switched on, paper inserted, cover closed, network cable plugged in? Does it print a self-test (hold the feed button while switching on)?
  4. Is the till computer or tablet in the same network as the printer (not guest Wi-Fi)?
  5. IoT box: in the IoT app, is the box and the device green?

For the administrator

  1. PDF engine: is wkhtmltopdf installed in the version Odoo expects (0.12.6 with patched Qt)? The Odoo log shows Unable to find Wkhtmltopdf or rendering errors.
  2. PDFs without layout, logo or fonts behind a proxy: wkhtmltopdf cannot load the CSS via the public address – set report.url to http://localhost:8069.
  3. Slow or failing large reports: check limit_time_real and the number of workers; very large PDFs are better split.
  4. Receipt printer by IP: when Odoo runs on HTTPS, the browser blocks connections to an http:// printer (mixed content) – use a printer with ePOS over HTTPS or an IoT box.
  5. Fix the printer's IP address (DHCP reservation in the router), otherwise it changes after a restart and the till looks in the wrong place.

What to send us

  • which document or device: e.g. "invoice INV/2026/00123" or "receipt printer at till 2";
  • the error message as text or screenshot, or the strange PDF itself;
  • for devices: model, how it is connected (USB, network, IoT box) and its IP address;
  • whether it ever worked – and what changed since (update, new router, new computer).
