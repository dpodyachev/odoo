What companies in Germany search for when they look for a CRM: 1,000 Google suggestions analysed

We collected about 1,000 Google autocomplete suggestions for "CRM" in Germany, Austria and Switzerland and sorted them by intent: free and price, integrations with Outlook and Excel, CRM for specific trades, GDPR, comparisons, understanding the term. What people really ask, what it means when choosing a CRM, where Odoo fits – and the traps when reading such data.

Before you choose a CRM – or write about it – it helps to know what people actually type into Google. Google does not publish search volumes for free, but its autocomplete suggestions show what is searched often enough to be suggested. We collected them for German-language searches in Germany, Austria and Switzerland and sorted them.

Data: Google autocomplete, language German, regions DE/AT/CH, collected on 18 November 2026 without login or cookies. 14 starting terms, each with the suffixes a–z and ä/ö/ü: 2,824 suggestions, 1,571 unique, of them 984 containing "crm" (without the Odoo terms). Suggestions show what is searched, not how often – the numbers below are counts of different suggestions, not search volumes.

What it means when choosing a CRM

  • "Free" is the largest group. Many small companies start with Excel, a free plan (HubSpot, Zoho) or open source. Free is fine as a start – check what happens at 3, 10 or 20 users, whether you can export your data and what the paid plan costs per user and month.
  • Excel and Outlook are the starting point. "crm excel vorlage" and "kostenloses crm für outlook" show where people come from. A CRM that imports the Excel list and works with Outlook (or Gmail) is adopted; one that requires a new way of working is often not.
  • Trade-specific CRM is in demand: craftsmen, estate and insurance agents, associations, tax advisers, doctors, car dealers. They do not need "a CRM" but their process: enquiry → appointment → quote → order → invoice. That is the point where CRM and ERP meet – "crm vs erp" is a frequent question for a reason.
  • GDPR is a buying criterion in Germany, not a footnote: "dsgvo konform" appears next to almost every big brand. The questions to ask: where are the servers, is there a data processing agreement (AVV, Art. 28 GDPR), how are contacts deleted, is self-hosting possible.
  • Comparisons are searched with the year ("crm vergleich 2026") – people want current lists. Most of them compare features; few compare total cost over three years, including introduction and training.
  • Many people first want to understand the term – "crm einfach erklärt", "beispiele", "nachteile". Whoever explains simply and honestly (including the disadvantages) wins trust before the sale.

Where Odoo fits

Odoo appears in the general CRM suggestions only a few times – the big names there are Microsoft (Dynamics), Zoho, SAP, HubSpot, Salesforce and monday. But people who search for Odoo CRM directly ask very concrete questions:

  • Experience and learning: odoo crm erfahrungen, odoo crm tutorial deutsch, odoo crm demo.
  • Price and licence: odoo crm kosten / preise, odoo crm free, one app free, open source, community edition.
  • Comparisons: odoo crm vs hubspot, vs pipedrive, vs salesforce, vs zoho, vs bitrix24.
  • Integrations: odoo crm outlook plugin, gmail integration, whatsapp integration, api, zapier.
  • Operation: odoo crm self hosted, on premise, docker – exactly the GDPR question from above.
  • The German specifics around it (from the Odoo suggestions in general): DATEV interface, GoBD, XRechnung and ZUGFeRD, VAT return. Here Odoo's advantage shows: CRM, quote, invoice and accounting in one system – if the German parts are set up properly.

Traps when reading such data

  • "CRM" is not always software. In Germany it is also Crew Resource Management ("crm leitsätze", "rettungsdienst", "luftfahrt"), the Centrum für Reisemedizin and the stock ticker of Salesforce ("crm aktienkurs"). Filter these out before counting.
  • Suggestions are not search volume. A suggestion means "searched often enough", nothing more. For numbers use Google Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account, ranges without ad spend), Google Trends or paid tools such as Sistrix or SE Ranking.
  • English mixes in ("crm software free", "crm system for marketing") – part of the German market searches in English.
  • Old years remain ("vergleich 2024") – suggestions change slowly. Collect again every few months and compare.
  • Regions differ little: Austria and Switzerland show almost the same list, plus "schweiz"/"österreich" variants and local partners.

Conclusion

People in Germany who look for a CRM ask about price, Excel and Outlook, their own trade and GDPR – far more than about features. Anyone choosing a CRM can use the same list as a checklist: what does it cost for our number of users, does it import our Excel list and work with Outlook, does it fit our process, where is the data.
