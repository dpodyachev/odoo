2. In short

This website sets no cookies, uses no tracking, no analytics, no advertising pixels, no embedded third-party content and no externally loaded fonts. There is no contact form. Your browser's local storage is not used either.

3. Hosting and server logs

The website is served via Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit, Cloudflare processes technically necessary data (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser type) to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in secure, stable operation). Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; a data processing agreement with standard contractual clauses is in place. I do not analyse this data myself.

4. Contact by e-mail or Telegram

If you write to me, I process your details (name, contact data, message content) to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR – pre-contractual measures, otherwise (f)). The data is deleted once the request is settled and no statutory retention obligations apply. E-mail is handled by Google Gmail (Google Ireland Ltd., Dublin). Telegram is operated by Telegram Messenger Inc. (Dubai, UAE); using it is voluntary and subject to Telegram's privacy policy – you can always e-mail instead.

5. Booking a call (Cal.com)

The “Book a call” button leads to Cal.com (Cal.com, Inc., USA). Only if you book an appointment there does Cal.com process your details (name, e-mail, requested time) under its own privacy policy. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR). An informal e-mail is enough.

7. Right to complain

You can lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, e.g. the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information Baden-Württemberg, Lautenschlagerstraße 20, 70173 Stuttgart, Germany.

8. Data processing for clients

If I get access to personal data of your company while supporting your Odoo system, we conclude a separate data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR).
