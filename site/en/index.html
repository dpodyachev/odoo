Mandatory since 2025

E-invoicing: XRechnung & ZUGFeRD

Since 1 Jan 2025 companies in Germany must be able to receive e-invoices; from 2027/2028 (depending on turnover) they must also send them in B2B. Odoo can do it – when configured properly.

  • ZUGFeRD / Factur-X (PDF with XML) and XRechnung (UBL/CII)
  • Leitweg-ID for public authorities, validator checks
  • Receiving and automatically capturing incoming e-invoices

Free · 5 questions · 2 minutes

E-invoicing check for Odoo

Is your Odoo ready for mandatory e-invoicing in Germany? Answer five short questions – you'll see right away where you stand and what to do.

1Receiving: Can you receive XRechnung and ZUGFeRD invoices and read the XML data – not just view the PDF?

Mandatory for all companies since 1 Jan 2025.

2Sending: Does your Odoo create customer invoices as ZUGFeRD/Factur-X or XRechnung?

Mandatory from 1 Jan 2027 for prior-year turnover above €800,000, from 1 Jan 2028 for everyone (B2B).

3Master data: Are VAT IDs, full addresses and – for authorities – Leitweg-ID or buyer reference maintained for your customers?

Missing mandatory fields are the most common reason for rejected e-invoices.

4Validation: Are your e-invoices validated against the standard before sending (e.g. with the KoSIT validator)?

A PDF that looks fine can still be an invalid e-invoice.

5Archive: Are incoming and outgoing e-invoices kept in their original XML in a GoBD-compliant way?

For e-invoices the XML is the original – not the PDF. Retention: 8 years.
