Accounting

DATEV export for your tax advisor

Journal entries and documents from Odoo in DATEV format – your Steuerberater imports them directly, no retyping.

  • Posting batches (DATEV format, EXTF) and document images
  • Charts of accounts SKR03 / SKR04, tax keys
  • Alignment with your tax office, test export before month-end closing

Mandatory from 2025

E-invoicing: XRechnung & ZUGFeRD

Since 1 Jan 2025 companies in Germany must be able to receive e-invoices; from 2027/2028 (depending on turnover) they must also send them in B2B. Odoo can do it – when configured properly.

  • ZUGFeRD / Factur-X (PDF with XML) and XRechnung (UBL/CII)
  • Leitweg-ID for public authorities, validator checks
  • Receiving and automatically capturing incoming e-invoices
