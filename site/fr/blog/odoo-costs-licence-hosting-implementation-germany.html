What Odoo really costs in Germany: licence, hosting and implementation – with a 3-year example

Cet article n’est pas encore disponible en français – voici la version anglaise.

"Odoo Kosten", "Odoo Preise", "Odoo kostenlos" – the most frequent price searches about Odoo in Germany. The current licence prices (Standard, Custom, One App Free, Light user, as of November 2026), when you need Custom, what Community costs in reality, hosting options, the implementation effort for a small company, a 3-year calculation for 5 users and the steps to estimate your own price.

The licence is the number everybody looks at first – and usually not the biggest one. The total consists of the licence, hosting, implementation and ongoing care. This article goes through all four with current numbers and a small calculation you can adjust.

Licence prices are taken from odoo.com/de_DE/pricing on 18 November 2026 (Odoo Online, per user and month). Odoo changes prices and discounts; check the price configurator before deciding, including whether VAT is added. Hosting and implementation figures are our assumptions for a small company – they are marked as such.

Licence prices (Odoo Online)

Odoo sells per user and month. The first 12 months are discounted for the users ordered at the start:

  • Portal users (customers who see their quotes and invoices) do not count as users.
  • Not included: Odoo.sh hosting, implementation services, maintenance of your own modules and in-app credits (SMS, contact enrichment, lead generation, AI scanning of bills).
  • Hosting on Odoo Online is included, with daily backups; the pricing page mentions storage up to 100 GB.

When Standard is not enough

  • External API – any connection where *your* system calls Odoo: your own scripts, a shop that is not Odoo, n8n/Zapier flows, a monitoring bot. Odoo's own integrations (payment, shipping, bank, VoIP) work in Standard.
  • Odoo Studio – building your own fields, screens and reports by mouse.
  • Several companies in one database.
  • Own server or Odoo.sh – Enterprise outside Odoo Online always means Custom.
  • One App Free switches to Custom automatically as soon as you install Studio or add companies – and of course as soon as you need a second app.

Community: €0 licence – what it means

Odoo Community is open source (LGPL): no licence, any number of users, your own server. What you pay instead is hosting, administration and the gaps you close with OCA modules. For a German small company the relevant differences:

Hosting

  • Odoo Online – included in the licence, no own server, but no own modules (except Studio) and no direct database access.
  • Odoo.sh – Odoo's platform for Enterprise with own modules; costs extra, needs Custom.
  • Own server (VPS) in Germany – for Community, or Enterprise with Custom. A small company (up to about 20 users) runs well on a VPS with 4 vCPU and 8 GB RAM; at European providers that is roughly 10–40 € per month. Add backups to a second location, monitoring and updates – that is the real cost, not the server.
  • GDPR: for any hosting you need a data processing agreement (AVV) with the host, and you should know where the data and backups are.

Implementation: the real cost

Implementation is where the money goes – and where it is saved. For a small company with standard processes (CRM, quotes, invoices, DATEV) we see roughly these blocks:

BlockDays (small company)What happens
Analysis and setup1–2processes, apps, company, SKR03/SKR04, taxes, users
CRM and sales1–2pipeline stages, quote template (DIN 5008), products and prices
Accounting and DATEV1–2DATEV settings, test month with the tax advisor, XRechnung
Data import0.5–2customers, products, open items from Excel or the old system
E-mail and integrations0.5–1Outlook/Gmail, mail plugin, website forms
Training and go-live1–2key users, first weeks with support
  • These are assumptions for a simple start – 5 to 11 days in total. Individual development, many interfaces or migration of history multiply it.
  • Multiply by the day rate of your partner. Ask for a fixed scope with a list of what is included rather than an open-ended estimate.
  • Odoo's own Success Packs are offered for companies under 50 employees; their prices are not on the pricing page.

Step by step: estimate your own price

  1. Write down the processes you want in Odoo (e.g. CRM → quote → invoice → DATEV) and the apps behind them.
  2. Count users: who works in Odoo daily (user), who only records time or expenses (check Light user), who is a customer (portal – free).
  3. Check the Custom triggers: Studio, external API (shop, n8n, own scripts, bots), several companies, own server. One yes = Custom or Community.
  4. Try it: an Odoo Online trial for Enterprise, or a Community test database on a small server – we use exactly that for our blog examples.
  5. List the gaps for Community: DATEV, UStVA, WhatsApp, bank import – each is an OCA module that someone has to install and maintain.
  6. Count credits: SMS, enrichment, OCR of bills – these are paid separately in both editions.
  7. Ask for a fixed-scope offer for the implementation and add hosting and care for 3 years. Only then compare.

Conclusion

Odoo is cheap to start and honest in price – but the licence is only one of four parts. For a small company the decision is usually: Standard on Odoo Online if you need no API and no own modules; Custom or Community on your own server as soon as integrations or own development come in. We calculate this with companies in Germany, Austria and Switzerland and show the setup on a test database before anything is bought.
